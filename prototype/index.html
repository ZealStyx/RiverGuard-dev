Design variants

RiverGuard
Four visual directions

One flood-risk monitoring platform, four design schools. Each variant is a complete, clickable prototype — monitoring stations, hazard map, forecast & risk, alerts & administration — rendered in a distinct visual language.

Information Architecture

Variant 01 — Rational

Pentagram / Tufte school. Black, white, and hairline rules. Data-dense tables, serif headings, monospace metrics. Built for operators who read many numbers at a glance.

Open variant
Modern Tool SaaS

Variant 02 — Tool

Linear / Vercel school. Deep dark surfaces, hairline borders, a single cyan accent, monospace chips. The developer-tool aesthetic for technical users.

Open variant
Editorial Minimalist

Variant 03 — Quiet

Kenya Hara / MUJI school. Off-white paper, large serif headings, generous whitespace, low density. Calm authority under pressure.

Open variant
Warm Humanist

Variant 04 — Human

Approachable, organic, earth-toned. Soft radius, warm shadows, terracotta and safety green. Built for residents and community readers.

RiverGuard · Design prototype — not operational software
RiverGuard supports decision-making; it does not issue official evacuation orders or replace LGUs and government agencies. No prediction, probability, or warning should be treated as operational or validated until the underlying data and models have been tested for their intended use.
