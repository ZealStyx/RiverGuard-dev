RiverGuard · Prototype — early development / architecture scaffold
RiverGuard supports decision-making; it does not issue official evacuation orders or replace LGUs and government agencies. No prediction, probability, or warning should be treated as operational or validated until the underlying data and models have been tested for their intended use.
